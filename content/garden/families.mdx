# Families

## Rosaceae

**Selected genera**

* <Gen n="Rosa" /> roses
* <Gen n="Fragaria" /> strawberries
* <Gen n="Malus" /> apples
* <Gen n="Prunus" /> cherries, plums, peaches, apricots, almonds(!)
* <Gen n="Pyrus" /> pears
* <Gen n="Rubus" /> raspberries and blackberries

**Features**

* Usually 5 petals and 5 sepals
* Many stamens
* Leaves often have serrated/toothed edges
* Usually stipules, little leafy flaps where the leaf stalk meets the stem

## Brassicaceae

Also called Cruciferae, "cross-bearing", after its four petals in a cross.

**Selected genera**

* <Gen n="Brassica" /> cabbage, broccoli, cauliflower, kale, Brussels sprouts (*B. oleracea*); turnips, pak choi (*B. rapa*)
* <Gen n="Armoracia" /> horseradish
* <Gen n="Eruca" /> rocket
* <Gen n="Eutrema" /> wasabi
* <Gen n="Nasturtium" /> watercress
* <Gen n="Raphanus" /> daikon, radish

**Features**

* 4 petals in a cross, 4 sepals
* Usually 6 stamens, 4 long and 2 short
* Contain glucosinolates, which turn into hot mustard oils when cut or chewed
