## Algorithms

| Algorithm | When empty | Onboard target | Tie breaker | Stop rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bounce` | Bouncing up/down. | Ignore | - | Stop at every floor. |
| `nearest` | Bouncing up & down. | Nearest onboard destination to the current floor. | First rider on board. | Stop only at requested destinations while occupied. |
| `popular` | Bouncing up & down. | Everyone on board vote up/down, then nearest rider on that side. | First rider on board. | Stop only at requested destinations while occupied. |
